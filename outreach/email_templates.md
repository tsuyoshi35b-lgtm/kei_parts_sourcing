# 英文の営業メール

目的は売り込みではなく聞き取り。「部品の依頼は月何件か」「手に入りにくい部品は何か」「今はどこから仕入れているか」を聞き、今困っている部品の無料見積もりを提案する。

## 送る前の注意
- 1社ずつ個別に送り、最初の1文を相手に合わせる。1日10社程度まで
- 署名に住所と配信停止の方法を入れる（CAN-SPAM）
- 「48時間以内に見積もり」「見つからなければ全額返金」は、実際に守れる場合だけ書く
- 名義は事業主体（妻）にそろえる

## ① 米国の軽トラ輸入業者向け

```
Subject: Sourcing kei truck parts from Japan for your customers

Hi [Name / the team at Company],

My name is [Your Name]. I run a small parts sourcing service in Osaka, Japan, focused on Japanese-market kei trucks (Carry, Hijet, Acty, Sambar, Minicab). [One line about them, e.g. "I saw that you also install plows and accessories, so you probably get a lot of parts questions."]

Before offering anything, I'd like to understand how dealers like you handle parts requests. Could I ask three quick questions?

1. Roughly how many parts requests do you get from customers each month?
2. Which parts are the hardest to find, or take the longest to arrive?
3. How do you source them today (US suppliers, online shops in Japan, a contact in Japan)?

What I can offer:
- Part identification by frame number and model code, using Japanese parts catalogs
- New genuine or quality Japanese-brand parts, sourced from suppliers in Japan
- One combined weekly shipment for all your orders, to keep shipping costs down
- A quote within 48 hours, with simple pricing: parts cost + a fixed handling fee
- A full refund if a part can't be found or doesn't fit as quoted

If there is a part you're struggling to find right now, just send me the frame number and the part name, and I'll quote it free of charge.

Thank you for your time.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Business Name], Osaka, Japan
[Email] | [WhatsApp]
[Business Address]
If you'd prefer not to receive further emails, just reply and let me know.
```

和訳（要旨）：大阪で軽トラ部品の調達サービスをしています。提案の前に3つ質問させてください。①部品の依頼は月何件か ②入手しにくい部品は何か ③今はどこから仕入れているか。車台番号からの部品特定、日本からの新品部品の調達、週一回のまとめ発送、48時間以内の見積もり、部品代＋固定手数料、見つからない・合わない時の全額返金を提供します。今探している部品は無料で見積もります。

## ② 欧州の小型トラクター輸入業者向け

```
Subject: Parts sourcing in Japan for your Japanese compact tractors

Hello [Name / the team at Company],

My name is [Your Name], and I run a small parts sourcing service in Osaka, Japan. I noticed that you import used Japanese compact tractors and also sell parts for Kubota, Yanmar, Iseki and other brands.

I'd like to ask how you handle parts that are hard to get outside Japan. Three quick questions:

1. Which models or parts do your customers ask for most often that you can't easily supply?
2. Roughly how many of these requests do you get each month?
3. How do you source them today?

I can identify parts by model and serial number using Japanese parts catalogs, source new parts from suppliers in Japan, and send your orders in one combined shipment. Pricing is simple: parts cost + a fixed handling fee, with a full refund if a part can't be found.

If you have a part you're looking for right now, send me the model, serial number and part name, and I'll quote it free of charge.

Kind regards,
[Your Name]
[Business Name], Osaka, Japan
[Email] | [WhatsApp]
[Business Address]
```

和訳（要旨）：日本製小型トラクターを輸入し部品も売っていると拝見しました。①入手しにくくよく頼まれる部品 ②その依頼の月件数 ③今の調達方法を教えてください。型式と機番から部品を特定し、まとめて発送できます。今探している部品は無料で見積もります。

## ③ フォローアップ（返信がない場合、5〜7日後に1回だけ）

```
Subject: Re: [original subject]

Hi [Name],

Just a short follow-up on my email from last week. If there's one part your customers are waiting on right now, send me the frame number (or model and serial number) and the part name. I'll come back with a price and delivery time within 48 hours, with no obligation.

Thanks again,
[Your Name]
```

和訳（要旨）：先週のメールの続きです。今お客様が待っている部品が1つでもあれば、車台番号と部品名を送ってください。48時間以内に価格と納期をお知らせします。義務はありません。

## 返信が来たら記録すること（data/inquiries.csv）
月の依頼件数、具体的な部品名と車台番号、今の仕入れ先と価格、待ち日数。具体的な部品の依頼が3件集まれば段階1の条件を満たす。
